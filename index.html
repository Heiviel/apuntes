How to use this app. It has five study tools plus this help. Everything works offline in your browser, and your progress is saved on this device only.
1Study notes›
  • Click a section title to open or close it. Each one starts with a highlighted Key idea and then the main points from your notes.
  • Words with a dotted underline are key terms. Hover over them (computer) or tap them (phone/tablet) to read a definition. Tap again to close it.
  • Press Mark as studied at the end of a section to track your progress. The bar at the top fills up as you go. You can undo it at any time.
2Timeline & links›
  • Events are listed in order and grouped by period (Restoration, Decline and crisis, Primo de Rivera, Second Republic).
  • Click an event to open its explanation. You will see Because of (what caused it) and Led to (what it caused). Click any of those links to jump to that event. Click the event again to close it.
  • Events directly connected to the open one get an orange outline.
  • Use the filters Where (Spain, Basque Country, Catalonia) and Theme (politics, nationalism, labour, army, economy, Church) to show only what you want to review. Choose All to clear a filter.
3Galaxy map›
  • Every star is an event. The oldest is near the centre and the newest on the outer edge. A bigger star has more connections. Colours show the period (see the legend at the bottom of the map).
  • Lines go from cause to effect.
  • Click a star to light up its connections and show its story in the card next to the map (or under it on a phone). Click empty space or the same star again to deselect. Use the links in the card to move along the chain.
  • Move a star: drag it. Its lines follow. Move the whole map: drag the dark background. Zoom: mouse wheel, or the + / − buttons. The ⟲ button resets the view.
  • Spread out overlaps pushes crowded stars apart automatically. Reset layout puts every star back in its original place. Your layout is remembered.
  • The Theme chips dim the stars that do not belong to a theme.
  • Show in timeline opens that event in the Timeline tab.
4Flashcards›
  • You see a question on the card. Click the card to flip it and see the answer. Click again to flip it back.
  • Then choose: I knew it removes the card from the deck, and Show again soon puts it back a few cards later.
  • The counter shows how many cards you have learned and how many are left. When the deck is finished, press Shuffle & restart.
  • Choose the deck at the top: All cards, Key terms or Facts & dates. Changing deck starts it again in a new random order.
5Trivia game›
  • Pick a mode: Quick round (10 questions), Survival (3 lives, ends when you run out) or Weak spots (only what you missed; it unlocks after your first mistake).
  • Question types change all the time: multiple choice, true/false, “which came first?”, “which event took place in…?” and Put in order (tap the four events from earliest to latest; Undo removes your last tap).
  • On a computer you can press 1–4 to answer and Enter to go to the next question.
  • After each answer you get the explanation and the section to review. Points: 10 per correct answer plus a bonus for streaks.
  • The game remembers what you get wrong and asks it more often, and avoids repeating recent questions. A topic counts as mastered after two correct answers in a row.
  • Quit ends the game and shows your results; answers given so far are kept. Reset my progress on the menu deletes all trivia statistics.
?Tips and troubleshooting›
  • Suggested routine: read a section in Study notes → follow its events in the Timeline or Galaxy → review with Flashcards → test yourself in the Trivia → repeat Weak spots until it is empty.
  • Theme: the button at the top right switches between light and dark mode.
  • Saved data (studied sections, galaxy layout, trivia progress) lives in this browser. It is lost if you clear site data or use a private window, and it is not shared between devices.
  • Phones: everything works with touch; the tab bar scrolls sideways.
